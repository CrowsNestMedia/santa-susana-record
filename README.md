# Santa Susana Record

Public record of the July 1959 partial meltdown at the Sodium Reactor Experiment, Santa Susana Field Laboratory, Simi Hills, California.

On July 13, 1959 an experimental reactor with no containment dome partially melted above Los Angeles. Thirteen of 43 fuel elements were damaged. About six weeks later the Atomic Energy Commission said a fuel element had parted and that no radioactive material was released. That sentence was false. The public did not hear "partial meltdown" until 1979.

This repository is the source of the record published by Crows Nest Media / Yom Ha Din. It is not a government site and it is not affiliated with Boeing, NASA, or the Department of Energy.

## What this record will not claim

Worker testimony is labeled testimony. The size of the 1959 release is labeled disputed. Independent estimates put the iodine-131 release hundreds of times above Three Mile Island; Boeing and the Department of Energy reject that magnitude. The claim that nothing was released does not survive the documents.
